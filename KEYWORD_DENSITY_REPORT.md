## 2. Analysis & Gap Identification

### 2.1 The "Exact Match" Gap
While "Gold Coast" and "Cardiologist" appear separately many times, the **exact phrase "Cardiologist Gold Coast"** is missing from the body text. This is a critical gap for local search ranking.

### 2.2 Semantic Depth vs. Keyword Frequency
The site has excellent **semantic depth** (mentioning TAVI, PCI, etc.), but the **frequency** of the doctor's name and primary title is slightly lower than the ideal benchmark (0.5% - 1.0%) for a personal authority site.

### 2.3 Procedure Keyword Dilution
Procedure keywords like "Stenting" and "Angiography" are currently below 0.15%. To be seen as a "Top Authority" for these specific services, we need to weave them more naturally into the descriptions.

## 3. Suggested Content Improvements

### A. The Hero Section (High Impact)
*   **Current:** "...interventional cardiologist on the Gold Coast..."
*   **Improvement:** "...leading **Cardiologist on the Gold Coast** and Senior **Interventional Cardiologist**..."
*   *Result:* Captures the exact primary search phrase immediately.

### B. The About Section (Authority Building)
*   **Current:** "Dr Shailesh Khatri is a highly experienced interventional cardiologist..."
*   **Improvement:** "As a premier **Heart Specialist on the Gold Coast**, **Dr Shailesh Khatri** has established a reputation for excellence in **Interventional Cardiology**..."
*   *Result:* Increases density for "Heart Specialist" and "Dr Shailesh Khatri."

### C. The Services Section (Technical SEO)
*   **Improvement:** Expand the "Coronary Angiography" and "Angioplasty & Stenting" descriptions to include the phrase **"Heart Attack treatment"** and **"Emergency Cardiology"** at least once more.
*   *Result:* Boosts the "Heart Attack" and "Emergency" keyword signals.

### D. The FAQ Section (Long-Tail Capture)
*   **Improvement:** Add a question: *"Why choose a private **Cardiologist on the Gold Coast** like Dr Khatri?"*
*   *Result:* Naturally embeds the primary target phrase in a high-value FAQ area.

## 4. Summary Recommendation
The website is structurally perfect, but the **content layer** needs a "keyword injection" to move from 0.2% density to the **0.5% - 0.8% "Sweet Spot."** 

**Next Steps:** I recommend applying these text-only refinements to the `dev` branch to bridge the density gap without changing the professional tone of the site.
