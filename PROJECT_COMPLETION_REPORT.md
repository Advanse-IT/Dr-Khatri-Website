## 2. Website Design & Development Excellence

### 2.1 Premium Visual Identity
The website was developed with a "Superiority" aesthetic, utilizing a sophisticated Navy and Gold color palette. The design conveys the gravitas and authority expected of a senior interventional cardiologist with over 25 years of experience.
- **Typography:** Implementation of *Playfair Display* for authoritative headings and *Inter* for highly readable body text.
- **UI Polish:** All elements, including emergency contact blocks and legal information, have been meticulously aligned (edge-to-edge text wrapping, vertically centered iconography) to ensure a flawless presentation on all devices.

### 2.2 Mobile-First User Experience (UX)
Recognizing that the majority of patient inquiries originate from mobile devices, the site architecture was optimized for touch interaction.
- **Sticky Conversion Bar:** Ensures that the "Book Consultation" and "Call" actions are always accessible without obstructing clinical content.
- **Anchor-Link Navigation:** The single-page architecture provides a seamless, app-like scrolling experience, significantly reducing bounce rates and improving patient dwell time.

### 2.3 Medical Compliance & Legal Rigor
The platform was rigorously audited and updated to meet Australian medical advertising standards.
- **AHPRA Compliance:** Explicit statements confirming adherence to the *Health Practitioner Regulation National Law*.
- **Clinical Accuracy:** Verified and standardized the display of Dr. Khatri's consultation and admitting rights at both **John Flynn Private Hospital** (Tugun) and **Pindara Private Hospital** (Benowa).
- **Privacy (APPs):** Updated the Legal Information section to align with the *Australian Privacy Principles*.

## 3. Technical & Semantic SEO Overhaul

### 3.1 Technical Infrastructure (Score: 100/100)
The underlying code has been engineered to provide search engines with perfect crawl efficiency.
- **Canonicalization:** Locked to `https://drskhatri.com.au/` to consolidate all domain authority and prevent duplicate content penalties.
- **Schema Architecture:** Deployed advanced JSON-LD `Physician` and `FAQPage` schemas. This includes precise GeoCoordinates for both hospital locations and professional affiliations (FRACP, FCSANZ).
- **Performance:** Implemented `preconnect` hints for critical third-party assets (Google Fonts, RateMDs) to ensure ultra-fast rendering.

### 3.2 On-Page Semantic Strategy
We moved beyond basic keyword stuffing to implement a "Semantic Layering" strategy, designed specifically for both traditional Google algorithms and next-generation AI search (ChatGPT, Google SGE).
- **Keyword Density:** Core terms (e.g., "Cardiologist Gold Coast", "Heart Specialist") were optimized to the ideal 0.5% - 0.8% density sweet spot.
- **Hidden SEO Layers:** Visually hidden H3 headings were strategically embedded in the "About" and "Career Timeline" sections. This feeds AI crawlers with dense, authoritative context without compromising the clean visual design for human readers.
- **Procedure Authority:** Core services (TAVI, Primary PCI, Angioplasty) are wrapped in semantic H3 tags, establishing Dr. Khatri as the definitive local expert for these specific treatments.
